## 📁 Featured Project: Spotify Financial Performance Analysis (2021 2025)
✅ Project status: Completed

**Research question**:
How did Spotify’s revenue, profitability, cash flow and debt position change between 2021 and 2025, and how can we avoid misleading visualizations when analyzing financial metrics of different scales?


> Interpretation:
> This visualization shows Spotify’s net income trajectory. Annual losses shrink consistently across years, crossing from negative to positive in 2024.
> Importantly, I chose to plot profit on its own separate chart, rather than putting it together with much larger revenue values — this prevents the trend from being visually “crushed flat” on the graph.
